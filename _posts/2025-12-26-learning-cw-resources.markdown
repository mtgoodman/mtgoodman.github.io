I don't have much insight here so providing resources which helped me.

## Copying
- [LCWO](https://lcwo.net) - website - Learn CW Online - This has many tools and options.  Great resource.
- [Morse Runnner](https://dxatlas.com/MorseRunner/) - Windows executable - Contest simulator - Good for general QSOs and introducing QRM/QRN.
- [Morse Mania](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/morse-mania-learn-morse-code/id1511042196)

### ICR
- [Morse ICR](https://apps.apple.com/lv/app/morse-icr/id6736839333) - iPhone app

## Sending
- [Morse Invaders](https://www.morseinvaders.com/) - Sending morse practice gamified as the classic Space Invaders arcade game.  This is a great blend of practice, pressure, and fun.  Created by [KE6EEK](https://www.qrz.com/db/KE6EEK)
- [VBand](https://hamradio.solutions/vband/) - Website used in conjunction with [USB Paddle Interface](https://hamradio.solutions/vband/) allowing you to connect your key/paddle to your computer.  

## Courses
- [Long Island CW Club](https://longislandcwclub.org/)
- [CW Ops Academy](https://cwops.org/cw-academy/)
- [Morse Code Ninja](https://morsecode.ninja/) - I only used video/audio practice files for copying but Kurt Zoglmann #### also offers and entire course.
